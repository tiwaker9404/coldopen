From a job link to a reply.

  1. Your résumé, once

    Upload it and name the role. It becomes your profile; every number is copied from the résumé word for word.

  2. Paste a job link

    coldopen reads the post and the company’s homepage: colors, logo, typeface, and the line or number they lead with.

  3. Check the film

    It answers one requirement from the post with your results. Rewrite any line and render it again in a minute.

  4. Send it to one person

    It suggests who to write to, finds their email, and opens it in Gmail already addressed. You see when they watch, and it reminds you when to follow up.

From reply to interview

Follow-ups come due sooner when they watch without replying. When they reply, you get a brief for the call and three questions to ask.

It can’t make things up.

A recruiter spots an invented claim instantly. So these rules are checked in code on every line, not just asked of the AI.

  • Their words are quoted, never paraphrasedThe line from their site or job post is copied exactly.
  • Your results are shown word for wordStraight from your profile, which came from your résumé.
  • No invented numbersA number that isn’t in their site or your profile gets the line rejected, including in lines you edit.
  • No AI-sounding fillerShort lines, and phrases like “unlock”, “seamless” and “revolutionize” are banned.
  • Readable on any brandColors are checked for contrast, and text is fitted so nothing spills over.

Built for job seekers. Works for sales too.

Applying for jobs

Any role with results you can count: engineering, data, product, design, marketing, customer success. Leave a current employer out of your profile with one field.

Selling a product

Paste a list of prospects and get one film each, with a CSV of share links for your CRM or sequencer. Or mention @coldopen in Slack.

Kill the renderer halfway. The film still finishes.

Every step is saved in Postgres by unkillable, a durable-execution engine built for this. A new worker picks up where the old one died and redoes nothing that finished. A test kills a real worker on every change. In its benchmark of 200 refunds with workers killed at random:

83customers refunded twice by a plain retry loop
0refunded twice with unkillable

Or run it on your own laptop, free.

You need Node 24 and ffmpeg. Add Groq’s free tier or a local model through Ollama for natural copy; without one, a built-in writer makes plain scripts that are always correct.

git clone https://github.com/tiwaker9404/coldopen && cd coldopen && pnpm install
# your profile, from your résumé
pnpm coldopen profile resume.pdf me.json --role "Data analyst"
# a film
pnpm film https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/acme/123 -p me.json

For a team: docker compose up runs the web app, API, renderer and database. See the README.
