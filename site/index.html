From a job link to a reply.

  1. Your résumé, once

    Upload it and name the role. It becomes your profile, and every number is copied from the résumé word for word.

  2. Paste a job link

    coldopen reads the post and the company’s homepage: colors, logo, typeface, and the line or number they lead with.

  3. Check the take

    The film answers one requirement from the post with your results. Rewrite any line and render a new take in a minute.

  4. Send it to one person

    It suggests who to write to, including alumni of your school and people from your past teams, finds their email, and opens it in Gmail already addressed. You see when they watch.

It can’t make things up.

A recruiter spots an invented claim instantly. So these rules are checked in code on every line, not just asked of the AI.

  • Their words are quoted, never paraphrasedThe line from their site or job post is copied exactly.
  • Your results are shown word for wordStraight from your profile, which came from your résumé.
  • No invented numbersA number that isn’t on their site or in your profile gets the line rejected, including lines you edit.
  • No AI-sounding fillerShort lines, and phrases like “unlock”, “seamless” and “revolutionize” are banned.
  • Readable on any brandColors are checked for contrast, and text is fitted so nothing spills over.

Kill the renderer halfway. The film still finishes.

Every step is saved in Postgres by unkillable, a durable-execution engine built for this. A new worker picks up where the old one died and redoes nothing that finished. In its benchmark of 200 refunds with workers killed at random:

83customers refunded twice by a plain retry loop
0refunded twice with unkillable

Or run it on your own laptop, free.

You need Node 24 and ffmpeg. Add Groq’s free tier or a local model through Ollama for natural copy; without one, a built-in writer makes plain scripts that are always correct. It works for sales too: one film per prospect, with a CSV of share links.

git clone https://github.com/tiwaker9404/coldopen && cd coldopen && pnpm install
# your profile, from your résumé
pnpm coldopen profile resume.pdf me.json --role "Data analyst"
# a film
pnpm film https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/acme/123 -p me.json

For a team: docker compose up runs the web app, API, renderer and database. See the README.
