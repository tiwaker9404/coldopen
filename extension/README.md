# coldopen for Chrome

A **Make film** button on job posts (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, LinkedIn, Workable, Wellfound and YC's job pages), plus a right-click "Make a coldopen film for this link" anywhere. A click opens coldopen with the post ready to turn into a film. The extension reads nothing from the page and sends nothing until you click; then it opens one link.

## Install (about a minute)

1. Download [coldopen-extension.zip](https://tiwaker9404.github.io/coldopen/coldopen-extension.zip) and unzip it.
2. In Chrome, open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the `coldopen-extension` folder.
4. Open any job post and press **Make film**, bottom right.

To point it at your own coldopen, change `APP` in `background.js`.
